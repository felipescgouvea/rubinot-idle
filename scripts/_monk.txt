'''Monk''' was the fifth [[vocation]] to be added to the game, coming out on the [[Monk Vocation Update]] after more than 25 years since the game's release. As masters of unarmed combat, Monks specialize in the use of [[Fist Fighting Weapons]]. Also, they are capable of supporting and healing allies while using martial arts and mystical abilities to defeat enemies. They have access to three unique mechanics: 

* [[Harmony]], which is stored by using [[Builder Spells]], and consumed with [[Spender Spells]] in order to deal devastating blows.
* [[Virtue]], granting passive bonuses. Also, whenever Harmony is built or spent, the lowest % [[HP]] member of the Monk's party is healed via [[Virtue Healing]].
* [[Serene]], the state where a [[Monk]] is the most powerful. When the state is activate, it will be indicated with a violet bar or circle close to your character on your game screen

Regardless of the chosen [[virtue]], all virtues grant monks the passive ability to heal themselves or an ally whenever they create or consume [[harmony]] without spending additional [[mana]] through [[Virtue Healing]].

==Advancement and regeneration==
===Stat increase per level===
After advancing to the next level, every player receives an increase in [[capacity]], maximum [[hitpoint]]s and maximum [[mana]].

'''Monks''' gain:
* 25 [[oz]] of capacity
* 10 [[hitpoint]]s
* 10 [[mana]]


===Regeneration===
As time passes, every player will regenerate lost [[hitpoint]]s and [[mana]], as long as they have eaten recently (see the page on [[Regeneration]] for more information).

'''Monks''' regenerate:
* 1 [[hitpoint]] every 7 seconds
* 2 [[mana]] every 4 seconds

'''[[Promotion|Exalted Monks]]''' regenerate:
* 1 [[hitpoint]] every '''5''' seconds
* 2 [[mana]] every 3 seconds


===Promotion===
When a monk reaches level 20, they can purchase a [[promotion]] and become an exalted monk with certain benefits.
